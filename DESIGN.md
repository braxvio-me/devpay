# Design System: DevPay

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Clear Morning Sky"**

DevPay lives in a pale, frosted sky. Every screen opens on a sky wash that fades into a misty canvas, and work floats on it as white cards lifted by soft blue-grey shadow. Blue is the only voice with volume: one featured job card per group, the primary CTA, the slider, and the haloed centre action wear the sky gradient; everything else is white, mist, and ink. Small, precise accents carry meaning: coral for what you saved or what is at risk, green for what is trusted or paid.

The density is that of a well-made consumer job finder: generous 20px margins, 24px between sections, compact 12-15px text, and a lot of air between rounded shapes. Interactive controls are pills; containers are softly rounded rectangles (20/24px); sheets and the nav round to 32px. Motion is physical and short: a press scales down (0.97 default) and springs back, with a selection haptic.

Light mode is the reference. Dark mode derives from the same hues: a deep blue-black canvas, the sky blue brightened for contrast, and shadows replaced by a one-pixel border.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Sky wash backgrounds (sky tint at top fading to mist canvas over ~360px).
- White cards, 20px radius, soft blue-grey shadow; featured cards 24px radius on the sky gradient.
- Pills for every control: chips, buttons, fields, search, segmented, slide-to-confirm.
- Pale "Apply ↗" fade corner on white cards; translucent "Apply ↗" tab on the featured card.
- Coral bookmark, green shield "Trusted", monogram/tile company logos in white rings.
- Floating frosted bottom nav with a haloed, 45°-rotated sky squircle as the centre action.
- System font only, hierarchy carried by 500/600 weights and tight negative tracking.

## Colors

A cool, near-monochrome sky-and-mist palette with three small semantic accents.

### Primary
- **Sky Blue** (sky-primary): selected chips, focus borders, radio dots, filter-active, verified decagram. The flat form of the brand blue.
- **Sky Gradient** (#5FBDEA to #3399CF, diagonal): featured job card, centre nav squircle, DevPay mark, premium "Pro" plan.
- **CTA Gradient** (#3A9DCB to sky-deep, diagonal): primary button, slide-to-confirm track, the CTA segmented variant. Reserved for the single strongest action on a screen.
- **Sky Ink** (sky-ink): blue text on pale blue: "Apply ↗" labels, soft buttons, blue status pills.
- **Sky Soft / Sky Wash** (sky-soft, sky-wash): tinted fills for soft buttons, list-row icon discs, filter button, apply fade.
- **Harbour Blue** (harbour-blue): active nav icon and label, list-row icon glyphs. A quieter, greyer blue so the nav does not shout.

### Secondary
- **Bookmark Coral** (bookmark-coral on coral-soft): active bookmark, unread dots on bell and Chats, field errors, destructive rows and danger buttons.

### Tertiary
- **Trust Green** (trust-green on green-soft): the "Trusted" shield, online dot, paid/complete status, premium feature checks.
- **Pending Amber** (pending-amber on amber-soft): pending/in-review status pills only.

### Neutral
- **Ink** (ink): primary text, icons on white.
- **Slate Secondary** (slate-secondary): company city, salary period, subtitles, "See all".
- **Slate Muted** (slate-muted): placeholders, inactive nav, chevrons.
- **Mist Canvas** (mist-canvas): screen background beneath the wash.
- **Mist Element / Selected** (mist-element, mist-selected): unselected chips, segmented track, image placeholders, radio rings, sheet handle.
- **Cloud Surface** (cloud-surface): cards, fields, icon buttons, sheets.
- **Hairline** (hairline): list-row separators, dividers.
- **Night set** (night-canvas, night-surface, night-element, night-border, night-text, night-primary, night-sky-ink): the dark-mode derivation of the same roles.

### Named Rules
**The Sky Gradient Rule.** Gradients come only from the sky family (card, CTA, screen wash, apply fade). Coral, green, and amber are always flat tints, never gradients.

**The One Featured Rule.** In any group of peers, at most one item wears the sky gradient (the first Recommended card, the Pro plan). The rest are white.

**The Semantic Accent Rule.** Coral means saved, unread, or destructive; green means trusted, online, or paid; amber means pending. None of them decorate.

## Typography

**Display Font:** System UI (SF Pro on iOS, Roboto on Android)
**Body Font:** System UI
**Label/Mono Font:** System UI with tabular numerals for money and countdowns

**Character:** One quiet native face; hierarchy comes from weight (500/600) and tightened tracking, never from a second family.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, 30-34px, tracking -0.8 to -1px, tabular): money figures: escrow balance, withdrawal amount, contract total, amount entry.
- **Headline** (600, 26-28px, tracking -0.6 to -0.7px): top-level tab titles (Messages, Contracts, Profile) and auth screen titles.
- **Title** (600, 17px, -0.2px): top bar titles, sheet titles, featured job titles; 16px for list-card job titles; 22px (Type.title) for in-content screen titles.
- **Section** (600, 15px, -0.1px): section headers ("Recommended", "Latest Post") beside a "See all »" link.
- **Body** (400, 14-15px): chip labels, list rows, field input, descriptive text (13px at 19px line height in choice cards).
- **Label** (500, 13px): field labels, salary lines, company names (15px/500).
- **Caption** (400, 12px): city, "Trusted", subtitles, timestamps.
- **Micro** (11px; 400 inactive, 600 active): bottom-nav labels.

### Named Rules
**The Two-Weight Rule.** 500 and 600 carry all hierarchy; 400 is reading text. 700 appears only inside monogram logos.

**The Tight Tracking Rule.** Tracking is zero or negative and tightens as size grows (-0.1px at 15px to -1px at 34px). Nothing in the system tracks positive.

## Layout

A single mobile column with 20px side margins and a 20px gutter (4-column grid), 24px between sections, and 16px card padding. Headers are 60px tall: avatar plus "My Location" on Home, back-title-action top bars elsewhere. The Home stack runs header, reminder panel (with the search bar nested inside), category pills, a horizontal Recommended carousel of 262 x 200 cards, then a vertical Latest Post feed. Chip rows scroll horizontally with 8px gaps. Content clears the floating nav with nav height + safe-area inset + 36px. Max content width is 800px on web.

## Elevation & Depth

Layered, ambient, and blue-tinted in light mode. Depth comes from the wash-to-mist background, white surfaces, and three shadow strengths, all tinted toward blue-grey rather than black. In dark mode, shadows are dropped and each surface takes a one-pixel night border instead.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Soft** (`0 6px 18px rgba(127,166,189,0.14)`): resting white cards, choice cards, plan cards.
- **Button** (`0 3px 8px rgba(91,124,143,0.14)`): icon buttons, fields, search bar, light buttons, segmented thumb.
- **Lifted** (`0 12px 22px rgba(63,167,218,0.28)`): sky-gradient elements only: featured card, primary CTA, slide-to-confirm, CTA segmented.
- **Nav** (`0 -6px 20px rgba(127,166,189,0.12)`): the bottom nav, cast upward.
- **Squircle glow** (`0 8px 14px rgba(63,167,218,0.38)`): the centre action and brand mark.

### Named Rules
**The Shadow-in-Light, Border-in-Dark Rule.** Light mode separates surfaces with blue-grey shadow; dark mode removes the shadow and draws a 1px night-border. Never both.

**The Blue Lift Rule.** Only sky-gradient elements get the blue lifted shadow; white elements never glow blue.

## Shapes

Two shape families. Controls are full pills (999px): buttons, chips, status pills, fields, search bar, segmented track and thumb, slider track and thumb. Containers are soft rectangles: 20px for white cards, choice cards, and multiline fields; 24px for featured cards and the reminder panel; 32px top corners for sheets and the bottom nav. Circles hold people and actions: avatars (white 2px ring), company logos (white disc with a hairline ring), icon buttons, bookmark, list-row icon discs. The one angular gesture is the brand squircle: an 18px-radius square rotated 45°, used for the nav centre action and the DevPay mark, with its icon counter-rotated upright. Inner tiles stay small: 12px for the search filter button, 6px for countdown digit tiles.

## Components

### Buttons
Confident pills that press down and spring back.
- **Shape:** full pill; 56px tall (lg) or 46px (md); label 16/14px at 600.
- **Primary:** CTA gradient, white label, lifted blue shadow (light mode).
- **Soft:** sky-soft fill, sky-ink label. **Light:** white with button shadow. **Dark:** ink fill, white label (inverts in dark mode). **Danger:** coral-soft fill, coral label. **Ghost:** transparent, slate label.
- **Press:** scale to 0.97 over 110ms, spring back (~260ms, damping 0.8), selection haptic. Disabled sits at 50% opacity; loading reads "Please wait…".

### Chips
- **Style:** pill, mist-element fill, no border; md 38px / 14px text, sm 30px / 12px text.
- **State:** selected fills sky-primary with white text; press scales to 0.94. Small chips are static tags on job cards.

### Status Pills
26px pill, 12px/500 text with optional 12px icon, five tones: blue, green, amber, coral, grey, each a soft fill with its saturated text.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 20px (white cards), 24px (featured, reminder panel).
- **Background:** cloud-surface; featured uses the sky gradient with white text and white-ring logos.
- **Shadow Strategy:** soft at rest, lifted for featured (see Elevation).
- **Border:** none in light; 1px night-border in dark.
- **Internal Padding:** 16px. Tappable cards scale to 0.985 with no haptic.

### Job Cards (signature)
Company row on top (40px logo, 15px/500 name, "City · Trusted" with a 12px green shield icon), bookmark at top right, title plus "GH₵ budget / period" below. The bottom edge carries the apply affordance: on white cards, a pale sky fade that occupies the right 52% and holds "Apply" plus an up-right arrow icon (Feather), both in sky-ink; on the featured card, a translucent white tab with a 20px top-left radius. Applicants appear as an overlapping avatar stack with a "+N" pill.

### Bookmark
34px circle. Inactive: white with a hairline ring and muted outline icon. Active: coral-soft disc with a filled coral bookmark. On the gradient: 35% white disc with a white icon. Press scales to 0.88.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white pill, 54px min height, 18px side padding, 19px muted leading icon, button shadow; 13px/500 slate label above with an 8px gap. Multiline becomes a 20px-radius box.
- **Focus:** 1px sky-primary border; caret in sky-primary.
- **Error:** 1px coral border plus 12px coral message below.
- **Search bar:** 52px white pill, leading search icon, trailing 40px filter tile (12px radius, sky-soft, turning sky-primary when filters are active).

### Segmented Control
48px pill track, 4px inset, with a sliding white thumb (spring ~300ms) and 14px/500 labels. The soft variant sits on mist-element; the CTA variant sits on the CTA gradient with a white thumb and white inactive labels.

### Slide to Confirm (signature)
58px pill on the CTA gradient with lifted shadow. A 120px pale thumb (#F2F9FD) holds the label; five chevrons fade from 35% to 100% white toward the end and dim as the thumb travels. Releasing past 72% (or flinging) confirms with a success haptic. Tapping the thumb also confirms, so the action does not depend on the gesture.

### Navigation
- **Bottom nav:** floating frosted panel (vertical wash from sky-wash tint to near-white), 32px top radius, white top hairline, upward nav shadow. Four tabs with 23px filled icons and 11px labels: harbour-blue at 600 when active, muted at 400 otherwise; coral unread dot on Chats.
- **Centre action:** 84px pale-sky halo ring around a 54px rotated sky squircle with lifted glow; medium impact haptic. Its icon changes by role: scan for freelancers, add for clients.
- **Top bar:** 60px; 44px white circular back button, centred 17px/600 title with optional verified decagram, 44px right slot.

### Sheets
Bottom sheet with 32px top corners, a 40 x 5 mist handle, 17px/600 title, and a 38% blue-black backdrop.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** start every screen with the sky wash fading into the mist canvas.
- **Do** make every interactive control a full pill and every container a 20px (or 24px featured) rounded rectangle.
- **Do** give exactly one item per peer group the sky gradient and lifted blue shadow.
- **Do** show company identity as a monogram or tile mark in a white ring, followed by "City · Trusted" with the green shield icon when verified.
- **Do** set money in GH₵ with tabular numerals and negative tracking.
- **Do** swap shadows for a 1px night border in dark mode.
- **Do** pair every press with the scale-and-spring response and a selection haptic.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface or weights outside 400/500/600.
- **Don't** track text positively or set labels in tracked uppercase.
- **Don't** use coral, green, or amber as decoration or as gradients.
- **Don't** put black or neutral-grey shadows on light surfaces; shadows are blue-grey or sky-tinted.
- **Don't** use real-brand logos for companies.
- **Don't** use square-cornered buttons, chips, or inputs.
